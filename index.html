The path to investment banking begins in first year

Spring Week applications open in September of your second year. By then your CV must already be competitive, which is the result of work you did the year before. CUNEF gives you the platform; we teach you how to use it.

1

Spring Week

Second to third year — apply in September

A week inside the bank. Performing well here fast-tracks you straight to a summer internship offer.

2

Summer internship

Third to fourth year

Ten weeks on a live desk. This is the stage that converts into a graduate offer.

3

Full-time offer

Final year

Most analyst seats are filled from the summer class, long before the graduate round opens.

Applying to any of these requires knowing how to apply: the right CV format, the particularities of each process, mastery of technical questions and a network of contacts. That is what the society is for.
